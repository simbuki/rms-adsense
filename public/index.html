The problem

RMS still sells airtime the way it did a decade ago.

Agency mark-ups
Minimum spend requirements and agency fees put broadcast advertising out of reach for many local traders.
No visibility
Sales staff have no shared, real-time view of which slots are open, pending, or already booked.
Double-booking risk
Manual, phone-and-paper processes leave room for the same slot to be sold twice.
How it works

Three steps, from open slot to paid invoice.

01
Browse & submit
Business clients filter open slots by station and type, then submit a booking request with details and a creative file.
02
RMS reviews
Sales staff review the pending queue and approve or reject each request from the admin dashboard.
03
Invoice & pay
Approval generates an invoice automatically. The client pays directly through the site to confirm the slot.
Coverage
